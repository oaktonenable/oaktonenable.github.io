Flagship Project

ISOWRITE

A smart assistive writing device engineered to counteract the involuntary tremors of Parkinson's disease — restoring one of life's most personal forms of expression.

Explore Prototype
Active Development
The Problem

PARKINSON'S
ROBS THE SIMPLE

Parkinson's disease affects the dopamine-producing neurons that control movement. Its most visible symptom — resting tremor — makes fine motor control profoundly difficult. Writing, once second nature, becomes a daily struggle.

Medication helps in early stages, but tremors often persist or worsen over time. And writing isn't just functional — it's deeply personal. Losing the ability to write a letter, sign your name, or keep a journal is a loss of identity and autonomy that millions face every day.

Our Solution

A PEN THAT THINKS
IN REAL TIME

Step 01 — Sensing
Tremor Detection
High-precision IMUs continuously monitor acceleration and rotation across all axes — capturing the user's tremor signature at hundreds of samples per second.
Step 02 — Processing
Signal Analysis
An onboard microcontroller separates involuntary tremor oscillations (3–8 Hz) from intentional writing movements using real-time filtering algorithms.
Step 03 — Actuation
Countermovement
The isolated tremor signal drives a precision stabilization mechanism that applies equal and opposite corrective displacement to the pen tip.
Step 04 — Output
Stable Writing
Smooth, controlled, legible handwriting — regardless of tremor severity. The patient writes naturally. The pen handles the rest, silently and continuously.
